About Pet Collections

🐾

About

Pet collections are organised groups of animals based on species, behaviour, housing needs, diet, and daily care requirements. Grouping pets by category helps people compare their needs and choose companions that fit their lifestyle.

📚

Facts & Myths

Fact: Different pets need different levels of space, attention, and enrichment. Myth: Small pets are always low-maintenance. Fish, rabbits, and hamsters still require careful daily care and suitable habitats.

💡

Why It Is Important

Understanding the differences between pets helps people prepare appropriate homes and provide responsible care. It also supports animal welfare and healthy relationships between pets and owners.

🧺

Collections Draft

Dogs are often active companions, cats need safe exploration, rabbits need room and hay, birds need enrichment and social contact, fish need clean water, and hamsters need secure habitats with opportunities to burrow.

✅

Actions by Consumer

Before adopting, research the animal’s needs, prepare a safe habitat, and plan for food, enrichment, veterinary care, and long-term responsibility.

Small routines, big difference

Pet care basics

Every species is different. Ask a veterinarian for advice tailored to your pet.

🥣

Feeding

Choose a complete, species-appropriate diet and keep fresh water available.

🧼

Grooming

Brush coats and check nails, ears, and teeth regularly using suitable methods.

🎾

Exercise & enrichment

Provide safe movement, play, and activities that engage the body and mind.

🧹

Hygiene

Keep sleeping, feeding, and toileting areas clean using safe products.

🩺

Veterinary care

Arrange routine check-ups and ask a veterinarian about preventive health care.
